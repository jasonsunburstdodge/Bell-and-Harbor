# Bell and Harbor | Lakeside Grill and Social

Responsive, navigable website prototype based on the supplied Bell and Harbor sitemap and concept assets.

## Run
Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve this folder using `python3 -m http.server 8080` and open http://localhost:8080.

## Pages
Home, Menus, Main Menu, Weekend Brunch, Craft Cocktails, Kids Menu & Kids Cocktails, Reservations, Private Events, Events Calendar, Contact & Directions, Order Online.

## Functional in this prototype
Responsive mobile menu; navigable page routes; menu sections; events category controls; browser form validation; date/time/party-size selection; accessible labels and navigation.

## Not live / required before launch
Reservation provider API, ordering/delivery provider, form submission backend, confirmed event feed, restaurant address/hours/phone, final approved menu items and prices, photography rights and approved real venue photography, analytics IDs, and production SEO/structured-data configuration. No simulated form submission is represented as a successful booking or delivered message.

The hero and food photography are cropped from user-provided concept imagery and should be replaced by approved individual photography for production. The official supplied logo is used as provided.
