From the paths along the water front, to the stone bell tower, buildings, and fountain strewn streets, Adriatica Village was inspired by the idylic coastal hamlet of Adriatica, Croatia.

Bell and Harbor sits at its center.

Whether you're dining, sharing drinks, or just enjoying the view, you'll feel like you're in an ancestral European village where the cobblestones are old and the stories are older. But this is where your friends are, and you get to make new ones.
